> ## Project Overview
> 
> The long-term research project "Niklas Luhmann – Theorie als Passion" at the Faculty of Sociology of the University of Bielefeld processes and edits Luhmann's scientific legacy in cooperation with the Digital Humanities of Bergische University Wuppertal.
> 
> ## Online Portal
> 
> Since 2019, the project operates the online portal niklas-luhmann-archiv.de and presents edited Zettelkasten notes as well as video and audio contributions with Niklas Luhmann, a complete publication list, and further information about the author and his work.
> 
> ## The Zettelkasten Digitization
> 
> A key focus is the digital reconstruction of the Zettelkasten, which comprises 90,000 notes. The Zettelkasten consists of two largely separate collections: Zettelkasten I (1952–1961) and Zettelkasten II (1961–1997), with the first collection comprising around 23,000 cards organized into 108 subject areas.
> 
> ## Project Timeline and Support
> 
> The project runs overall from 2015 to 2030 and is funded by the North Rhine-Westphalia Academy of Sciences and the Arts.
