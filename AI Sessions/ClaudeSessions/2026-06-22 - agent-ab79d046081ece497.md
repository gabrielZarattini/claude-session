> 342	### 5.1 Threat Model (STRIDE)
> 343	
> 344	| Component | Threat | Type | Severity | Mitigation |
> 345	|-----------|--------|------|----------|------------|
> 346	| `hyperframes-render` (Puppeteer) | HTML/JS de tenant executa código arbitrário → RCE / SSRF / leitura de env / pivô de rede | Elevation of Privilege / Information Disclosure | **Critical** | **Sandbox per-render** (container efêmero, `cap_drop`, `no-new-privileges`, rede restrita egress-only, ZERO credencial de tenant no env); `/security-review` obrigatório; bloquear render multi-tenant até selado (OTD-VS-006 · FR-VS-025) |
> 347	| `open-design` daemon (compartilhado) | BYOK de um tenant resolvido do config GLOBAL `/app/.od/media-config.json` → quota/receita/risco vazam cross-tenant (fraude por design) | Information Disclosure / Tampering | **Critical** | Re-arquitetura per-user (OTD-VS-002): daemon per-job / `od` stateless / container por tenant; manter single-tenant EXPLÍCITO até selar; gate de GA (FR-VS-007) |
> 348	| `design.mcorch.com` (CF→origin) | Superfície exposta com `OD_API_TOKEN=development_token` + origins abertos → acesso não-autenticado ao editor | Spoofing | High | `OD_API_TOKEN` forte rotacionado + `OD_ALLOWED_ORIGINS=https://login.mcorch.com` + CF WAF Skip rule (libera não-browser legítimo sem abrir geral); confirmar via origin `--resolve` (curl cru retorna challenge) (FR-VS-003 · NFR-VS-012) |
> 349	| `video-faceless-render` / `-hyperframes-render` | Acesso não-autenticado dispara render caro | Spoofing | High | JWT validation no entrypoint (§5.3) antes de qualquer lógica (FR-VS-001/002) |
> 350	| mcoCoins deduction | Bypass client-side / mint / drain | Tampering | **Critical** | `deduct_mco_coins` RPC server-side ONLY; nunca client-side; refund crédito-positivo idempotente (R2 · FR-VS-009/011) |
> 351	| `video_renders` / `video_assets` | Leitura cross-tenant de renders/assets | Information Disclosure | **Critical** | RLS `user_id = auth.uid()` default-deny; poll tenant-bound (404 p/ render alheio) |

> 358	### 5.2 RLS Policy Definitions
> 359	
> 360	```sql
> 361	-- video_renders (default-deny + tenant isolation)
> 362	ALTER TABLE video_renders ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;
> 363	
> 364	CREATE POLICY "video_renders_select_own"
> 365	  ON video_renders FOR SELECT
> 366	  USING (user_id = auth.uid());
> 367	
> 368	CREATE POLICY "video_renders_insert_own"
> 369	  ON video_renders FOR INSERT
> 370	  WITH CHECK (user_id = auth.uid());
> 371	
> 372	-- UPDATE de estado/refund é server-side (service-role) — sem policy de UPDATE p/ authenticated.
> 373	-- RESTRICTIVE no-delete (financeiro/auditável): clientes não apagam render (refund/ledger).
