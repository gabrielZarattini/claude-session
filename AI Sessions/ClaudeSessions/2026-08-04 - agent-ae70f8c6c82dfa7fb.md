**Result: NO exploitable vulnerabilities found.** All seven files were read in full and each attack surface was verified against the reference implementations. This is a clean result, not an absence of effort — below is what was checked and why each avenue closes.

### Tenant isolation — clean
`/home/gcrUX/htdocs/constellation-orchestra/supabase/migrations/20260804150000_spaces_node_threads.sql`
- RLS enabled; **SELECT-own only** (`owner_id = auth.uid()`), no INSERT/UPDATE policy ⇒ default-deny for `authenticated`/`anon` (writes are service-role only, matching `generations` in `20260702190000`). RESTRICTIVE `FOR DELETE USING (false)` mirrors the financial family.
- The composite FK **is** genuinely tenant-binding: `(project_id, owner_id) → vm_canvas_projects(id, user_id)`, and the anchor `vm_canvas_projects_id_user_unique` really exists (`20260707220000:13`). Both referencing columns are `NOT NULL`, so a cross-tenant pair is unrepresentable even under a service-role bug.
- `project_id` cannot be spoofed: `spaces-agent-chat/index.ts:173-179` resolves the project with `.eq("id", projectId).eq("user_id", user.id)` and 404s otherwise — identical to the `canvas-execute`/`lead-score` ownership-404 shape.
