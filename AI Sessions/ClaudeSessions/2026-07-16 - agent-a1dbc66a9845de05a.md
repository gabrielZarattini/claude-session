Critically, the DROP targets the 21-arg — a *different* signature from the live 26-arg — so a failed re-run never removes the live function; asset registration cannot end up dropped-but-not-recreated. Not strictly idempotent (`CREATE`, not `CREATE OR REPLACE`), but it fails safe.
