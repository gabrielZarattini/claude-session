> 187	
> 188	- **Cards:** Glass morphism — `backdrop-blur-sm`, `--border-subtle`, shadow `0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`. Inspector de cada nó tipado e wizard de consentimento usam glass surface.

> 191	- **Loading states:** Skeleton shimmer (nunca spinners para áreas de conteúdo); render assíncrono via webhook/poll mostra progresso real.
> 192	- **Toasts:** `sonner` — success = violet, error = red (HTTP 402 / Identity Gate fail / consentimento revogado), info = cyan. Mensagens de UI em **Português (Brasil)**.

> 351	### 5.1 Threat Model (STRIDE por componente)
> 352	
> 353	| Component | Threat | STRIDE Type | Severity | Mitigation |
> 354	|-----------|--------|-------------|----------|------------|
> 355	| `generate-voice` / `canvas-execute` / `avatar-identity-train` | Acesso não-autenticado a síntese paga | Spoofing | High | JWT validation no entry-point (§5.3); 401 sem token válido |
> 356	| `avatar_identities` / `voice_profiles` | Leitura cross-tenant de PII biométrica (weights/voiceprint de outro tenant) | Information Disclosure | **Critical** | RLS own `auth.uid() = user_id` default-deny; FMEA-011; storage prefixo `user_id/` + signed URL curta (NFR-AC-013) |
> 357	| `deduct_mco_coins` (avatar render) | Bypass client-side da dedução | Tampering | **Critical** | RPC server-side only; `AVATAR_VIDEO_COST` ≠ `VIDEO_COST`; zero dedução no cliente (NFR-AC-019) |
> 358	| BYOK key resolution | Fan-out de conta-mestra (service bureau) | Elevation of Privilege | **Critical** | Invariante anti-conta-mestra: toda chamada resolve `auth.uid()`; grep audit zero fan-out (FR-AC-006); Modelo A estrito |

### 4. Identity-Verification (Self-Clone) Gate — Exact Behavior

**Source: `/home/gcrUX/htdocs/constellation-orchestra/docs/bok/avatar-clone-ai/04-frd.md` §2.7; 05-sdd.md §4**

**Gate Definition (verbatim):**
"Prova que o user clona **A SI MESMO** antes de gerar weights (selfie-liveness vs fotos de treino OU attestation legal); falha → recusa treino + não persiste artefato" (FR-AC-032, 04-frd.md:111)

**Selfie-Liveness vs Training Photos:**
- **Input:** training photos (≥20 for Soul ID, FR-AC-012) + liveness selfie
- **Check:** ArcFace embed of liveness selfie must cosine-match the embedding derived from training photos (at verification threshold, empirically calibrated)
- **Failure behavior:** HTTP 403 returned; no weights persisted in `avatar_identities`; training aborted

**When Blocks (HTTP 403, no artifact persisted):**
1. Selfie-liveness cosine < verification threshold
2. User attempting to clone third-party private individual (TikTok bane syntético)
3. `persona_type` cannot be certified as `self_clone`

**Persona Type Field:**
- Column `persona_type` in `avatar_identities`: "IN ('self_clone','fictional')" (04-frd.md:306)
- "dirige templates de compliance distintos" (FR-AC-032, 04-frd.md:111)
- Self-clone = stricter disclosure; fictional = lighter disclosure (as creative work, EU AI Act Art. 50 exemption)
