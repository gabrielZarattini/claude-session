- **Costs don't kill it**: transaction costs explicitly do NOT explain the basis (edge survives fees); margin frictions and market segmentation do.
- **Perpetuals**: positive funding (longs pay shorts) is typical; a short-perp carry trade has documented high Sharpe ratios (Christin et al. 2022, cited approvingly) — but perpetuals don't converge to spot, so the arb isn't strictly enforced.
- **Tail risk timing**: carry is right-skewed and predicts short-futures liquidations (10% carry rise → +22% of OI liquidated next month), i.e. drawdowns cluster exactly when trend-followers bid futures up.
