Key takeaways for the TradeUX research question (intraday momentum / short-directional, item 1 of the brief):

1. Time-series momentum is real in BTC at a 30-min horizon (first half-hour predicts last half-hour), in- AND out-of-sample across 5 exchanges — but the effect is small (R² ~1-3%).

2. Cost reality check, directly load-bearing for the spot-vs-futures decision: breakeven costs are only 3/7/10 bps, BELOW the 25 bps Bitstamp spot fee, so the strategy is unprofitable on spot. It only survives with leverage (breakeven rises to 29/64/96 bps at 10:1). This corroborates the project's internal finding that fees kill intraday strategies on spot and that the math only changes with low-fee venues.

3. Skeptical nuance on shorting the fall: the momentum signal is materially WEAKER on down moves (R² 0.11-0.89% when the first half-hour is negative vs 1.89-3.19% when positive). The paper's celebrated "downturn benefit" is from market-timing/going-to-cash beating buy-and-hold in bear years (2014/2015/2018), NOT from a symmetric short edge. Useful counter-folklore evidence.
