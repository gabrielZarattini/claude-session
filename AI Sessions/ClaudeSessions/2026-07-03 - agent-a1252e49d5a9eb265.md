>    ```typescript

> 
> 3. **Handle rate limits**: Configure exponential backoff for retries:
>    ```typescript

>      maxRetries: 5,
>      retryBackoff: 2000,

> 
> 5. **Cache motion and style IDs**: Fetch and cache available motions/styles at startup:

>    const motions = await client.getMotions();
