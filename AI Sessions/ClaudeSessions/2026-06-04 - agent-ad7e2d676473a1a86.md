- **The injected `target` is not attacker-controllable into a dangerous href.** `target` is either `${supabaseUrl}/functions/v1/process-affiliate-link?link_id=${link.id}` (server env + DB-generated UUID) or `buildMlProductUrl(p.mlb, affiliateTag, mattTool)` where `p.mlb` is a hardcoded catalog constant and `affiliateTag`/`mattTool` go through `URL.searchParams.set()` (percent-encoded) on a fixed `https://www.mercadolivre.com.br` base (`_shared/affiliate.ts:26-34`). It cannot break the attribute or inject a tag, and it originates from the AI's own article, not from a request.

Confidence this is NOT exploitable: 9/10.
