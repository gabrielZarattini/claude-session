> 181	## 5. Security Design
> 182	
> 183	### 5.1 Threat Model (STRIDE)
> 184	
> 185	| Component | Threat | Type | Severity | Mitigation |
> 186	|-----------|--------|------|----------|------------|
> 187	| `design-job-enqueue` | Unauthenticated invocation | Spoofing | High | JWT validation at entry, before any logic |
> 188	| `design_jobs` / `_artifact_refs` / `_reviews` | Cross-tenant data read | Information Disclosure | Critical | RLS `user_id = auth.uid()` default-deny on every table |
> 189	| mcoCoins deduction | Client-side bypass / double charge | Tampering | Critical | Server-side `deduct_mco_coins` RPC; deduction before the job row insert |
> 190	| `mcorch-design-daemon` (`od`) | Public access to the design daemon | Spoofing / DoS | High | Daemon bound to the internal Docker network only — never a public port |
> 191	| Per-user AI credentials | One tenant's key used for another's job | Information Disclosure / Tampering | Critical | Bridge runs `od` with a per-user `OD_MEDIA_CONFIG_DIR`; credentials never shared across jobs |
> 192	| `design-bridge` | Compromised worker reaches all tenants' data | Elevation of Privilege | High | Worker holds the service role — runs only on the Sovereign host, no inbound ports, least-privilege filesystem |
> 193	| `design-review-access` | Forged or guessed review token | Spoofing | High | 256-bit random token, unique-indexed, time-limited, revocable; read + decision only |
> 194	| `design-assets` bucket | A user reads another tenant's render | Information Disclosure | High | Private bucket; short-lived signed URLs scoped to the owner |
> 195
